Where do I get yolo26n.onnx?

Ultralytics ships PyTorch weights, not a pre-built browser model — export one locally (~2 minutes), then upload it in the panel on the right. Nothing leaves your machine; inference runs entirely in this tab.

pip install ultralytics
yolo export model=yolo26n.pt format=onnx imgsz=640 opset=17 simplify=True

This produces yolo26n.onnx with an end-to-end, NMS-free output of shape (1, 300, 6) — this demo's decoder expects exactly that layout. Larger checkpoints (yolo26s/m/l/x.onnx) work too, just slower per frame.
